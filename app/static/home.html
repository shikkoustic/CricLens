Which shot was that?

The joint sequence goes to a classifier trained on eight strokes. You get the call and how certain it is — never a bare guess.

Then

How good was it?

A scorer trained on coaches' ratings puts a number on the technique, and shows where it sits against the strokes in the reference set. Stride and hand speed come straight off the footage when the crease is clear enough to measure.

About

CricLens analyses a single cricket delivery end to end — striker detection, pose tracking, shot classification, technique scoring and coaching feedback. Everything runs on your own machine; clips are never uploaded anywhere.
